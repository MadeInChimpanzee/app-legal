What it does

PICK YOUR PROJECT

Choose a garment: skirt, dress, blouse/shirt, pants, jacket or a child's garment. Choose a size (S, M, L, XL) and your fabric width — 90, 110, 115, 140 or 150 cm (36, 44, 45, 54 or 60 in). See the yardage instantly, in both yards and meters.

ACCOUNTS FOR THE HARD PARTS

With nap / one-way fabrics (velvet, corduroy, directional prints) add extra for a single-direction layout. Plaid and stripe matching adds fabric for repeat matching, based on the garment's major pattern pieces and your fabric's repeat length. On-fold vs single-layer cutting. Your own safety margin for shrinkage, mistakes and matching.

SEE THE MATH

A clear breakdown shows base fabric plus each adjustment, so you understand where the number comes from. Amounts are rounded up to the next 1/8 yard or 0.1 m — the amount you'd actually buy. Tap Copy to drop the result into your notes or shopping list.

QUICK REFERENCE

A standard fabric-requirement chart for every garment and fabric width, based on published Misses charts and pattern-envelope ranges.

HONEST ABOUT ESTIMATES

These are approximate. Every pattern is different — always check your pattern's own fabric requirement before you buy. SewYardage gets you close and helps you sanity-check the envelope. Buy once.
