Details

GolfWHS turns your rounds into a Handicap Index and the exact stroke numbers you need on any course - fast, private, and offline.

Log a round with your adjusted gross score, the Course Rating and the Slope Rating, and GolfWHS instantly calculates the Score Differential for you. Once you have a few rounds, it computes your Handicap Index using the World Handicap System method: the lowest differentials from your most recent 20 rounds, with the standard adjustments applied.

Heading to a new course? Enter the tee's Slope, Course Rating and Par to get your Course Handicap, then pick an allowance (100%, 95%, 90% and more) to get your Playing Handicap. Copy any result with one tap.
