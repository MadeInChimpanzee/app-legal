Frequently Asked Questions

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?
No. Editing happens entirely on your iPhone. Photos are never uploaded or sent to the developer.

How do I color only part of the photo?
Your photo opens in black & white. Brush over an area with one finger to bring its color back. Pinch to zoom in for fine detail (the brush stays the same size on screen, so it paints more precisely when zoomed in), and drag with two fingers to move around. Use the Eraser to send an area back to black & white, and Undo/Redo to step through changes. Invert swaps it around: brushed areas turn gray and the rest stays in color.

I closed the app in the middle of editing. Is my work lost?
No. Your current edit is kept as a draft on your iPhone. Next time you open ColorTouch it asks “Continue your last edit?” — choose Continue to pick up where you left off, or Discard to delete it. (Undo history is not kept.)

“Save” says photo access is off.
ColorTouch needs permission to add photos to save your picture. Open iOS Settings > ColorTouch > Photos and allow “Add Photos Only” (or Full Access), then tap Save again.

Is the saved picture full resolution?
Yes. Your edit is applied to the original photo at its full resolution and color.

Is this a subscription?
No. Editing is free. Saving and sharing are unlocked by a single one-time purchase — there is nothing to cancel or renew.

I bought ColorTouch but it asks me to pay again.
Tap “Restore Purchases” on the purchase screen, or in Settings inside the app, while signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

I need a refund.
Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. Refund requests go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
