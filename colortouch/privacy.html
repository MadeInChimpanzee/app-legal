Privacy Policy

App: ColorTouch  /  Last updated: October 30, 2026

ColorTouch (the “App”) turns a photo black & white and lets you brush color back in. It is designed to work entirely on your device. The App does not collect personal information and does not send your photos or data anywhere.

1. Information We Collect

The App collects no personal information — no name, email address, phone number or device identifier. There is no account and no sign-in.

2. Your Photos

You choose a photo with the standard iOS photo picker, or take one with the camera. The App receives only the single image you pick and edits it entirely on your device. The App does not browse, scan or index your photo library, and your photos are never uploaded or transmitted anywhere. Because the system picker hands over just the one image you choose, the App does not request access to read your photo library.

3. Permissions

Camera — used only when you tap “Take Photo”, so you can take a picture to edit. Camera images are processed on your device and are never transmitted.

Photos (add only) — requested only when you tap “Save”, so the App can add your finished picture to your photo library. This permission lets the App save new images; it cannot read your existing photos.

You can change either permission at any time in iOS Settings. The App does not access your location, contacts, microphone or Health data.

4. Data Stored on Your Device

So that you don’t lose work if the App is closed, the photo you are currently editing and the areas you have colored are kept as a draft on your device only, in the App’s own Application Support folder (excluded from iCloud backup). The draft is deleted when you save the picture, when you discard your edits, or when you choose “Discard” on the next launch. The App also stores a few settings (for example, whether you have finished the introduction). Deleting the App deletes all of this data.

When you share a picture, it is created on your device and handed to the standard iOS share sheet; where it goes from there is your choice and is handled by the app or service you pick.

5. No Network Connections, Analytics or Ads

The App does not send your data to the developer or to any server. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. The only online communication is with Apple’s App Store, for purchases (see below), which is handled by iOS.

6. In-App Purchase

ColorTouch is free to download and edit with. Saving and sharing are unlocked by a one-time in-app purchase (no subscription), processed by Apple through the App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple, and the developer never receives your credit card number or any other payment details. Apple’s handling of purchase data is described in Apple’s own privacy policy.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children. Its age rating is shown on its App Store listing. Because the App collects no data, it collects no data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be revised as the App changes or as required by law. Any material change will be reflected on this page with a new “last updated” date.
