Details

Gold is a fast, precise scoring app for target archers — built to be reliable on the shooting line and correct to the ring.

Score every arrow the way you like: tap the big one-handed pad, or plot each arrow on the target face with a floating aim reticle so your finger never hides the shot. Your end total, running score, average and 10s/X are always in view.
