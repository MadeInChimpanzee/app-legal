Details

Kotoba helps you learn essential beginner Japanese vocabulary, built around JLPT N5 — the perfect start for anyone learning Japanese or planning a trip to Japan.

Nearly 800 hand-picked words, each with the Japanese spelling, kana reading, romaji, English meaning, and a natural example sentence. Tap to hear every word and example read aloud by your iPhone's Japanese voice.
