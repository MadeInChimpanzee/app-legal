Privacy Policy

App: HitchCal  /  Last updated: November 2, 2026

HitchCal (the “App”) is designed to work on your device. The App does not collect personal information and does not send your data anywhere. This policy explains how information is handled.

1. Information We Collect

The App collects no personal information — no name, no email address, no phone number, no device identifier. There is no account, no login and no sign-in of any kind.

2. Where Your Data Is Stored

Everything you create in the App — your entries, records and settings — is stored only on your device. It is never uploaded to the developer or to any third party. If you delete the App, this data is deleted with it.

3. Network Connections

The App has no server of its own and does not send your data anywhere. It works offline. The only time it leaves the App is when you choose to open a link, such as the App Store page.

4. No Analytics, Advertising or Tracking

The App contains no third-party analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no tracking technology. No usage data or behavioural data is shared with anyone.

5. Other Sensitive Data

The App does not access your photo library, camera, motion sensors, contacts or microphone, and does not request or use location data or Health data.

6. Payment

HitchCal is a paid app purchased once from the App Store. There are no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; the developer never receives your credit card number or any other payment details. Apple’s handling of purchase data is described in Apple’s own privacy policy.

7. Children

The App’s age rating is shown on the App Store. The App does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be revised as features or laws change. Any revision is posted on this page with a new date.
