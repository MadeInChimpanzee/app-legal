よくあるご質問

データはどこに保存されますか？
記録はすべてお使いの端末内にのみ保存され、外部には送信されません。アプリを削除するとデータも削除されます。

機種変更でデータを引き継げますか？
設定のバックアップでファイルに書き出し、新しい端末で復元できます。

サブスクリプションですか？
いいえ。有料機能は買い切りの App内課金です。設定の「購入を復元」から復元できます。

FAQ

Where is my data stored?
Your records stay on your device and are not sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

How do I move to a new iPhone?
Export a backup file in Settings, then restore it on the new iPhone.

Is this a subscription?
No. Paid features are a one-time in-app purchase. You can use Restore Purchases in Settings.

How do I choose my city or pressure unit?
Choose a nearby city from the list in Settings. Your location is never used. You can also switch between hPa and inHg there.

Does the app diagnose my condition?
No. It is for tracking only. Talk to a doctor about any symptoms that concern you.
