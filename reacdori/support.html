FAQ

Why can’t I record?
Simultaneous front and back recording requires iPhone XS or later (A12 Bionic or newer). Please also allow camera and microphone access in the Settings app.

Where are my videos saved?
Recordings are processed on your device and saved to your Photos (Camera Roll). Nothing is uploaded.

Is there a subscription?
No. DualShot is a one-time purchase with no subscription and no ads.
