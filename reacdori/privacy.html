Privacy Policy

App: DualShot (リアク撮り)  /  Last updated: July 8, 2026

DualShot (the “App”) is designed with your privacy in mind. Our core principle is that the App does not collect any personal information and does not send your data anywhere. This policy explains how the App handles information.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect any personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number. No account or sign-in is required.

2. Camera and Microphone

The App uses your device’s cameras and microphone only to record video inside the App. All recording and video processing happen entirely on your device. Your camera and microphone feeds are never transmitted to the developer or any third party.

3. Photos

With your permission, videos you record are saved to your device’s Photos (Camera Roll). The App only adds videos you create; it does not read or upload your existing photos or videos.

4. Data Storage

Your recordings and settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to or stored on the developer’s or any third party’s servers. Deleting the App removes its data from your device.

5. Location, Analytics, and Advertising

The App does not access your location and does not use any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies.

6. Purchases

The App is a one-time paid purchase made through the App Store. Payment is processed by Apple, and the developer never receives your payment details. Please refer to Apple’s Privacy Policy for how purchases are handled.

7. Children

The App follows the age rating shown on the App Store.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as features or laws change. Significant changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.

9. Contact

リアク撮り（以下「本アプリ」）は、利用者のプライバシーを尊重して設計されています。本アプリは、個人情報を収集せず、外部に送信しないことを基本方針としています。

本アプリは、氏名・メールアドレス・電話番号などの個人を特定できる情報を一切収集しません。アカウント登録やログインは不要です。

録画データや設定はすべて利用者の端末内にのみ保存されます。開発者や第三者のサーバーに送信・保存されることはありません。アプリを削除するとデータも削除されます。

5. 位置情報・解析・広告

本アプリは、位置情報を取得せず、第三者の解析ツール・広告SDK・トラッキング技術を一切使用していません。

本アプリは App Store を通じた買い切り（有料）アプリです。決済は Apple が処理し、開発者が支払い情報を受け取ることはありません。
