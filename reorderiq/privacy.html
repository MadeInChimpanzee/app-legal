Privacy Policy

App: ReorderIQ  /  Last updated: July 29, 2026

ReorderIQ (the "App") is designed with your privacy in mind. The App's core policy is that it does not collect personal information and does not transmit any data externally. This policy explains how the App handles information.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect any personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number. No account or sign-in is required to use the App.

2. Where Your Data Is Stored

Everything you enter — SKUs, demand figures, costs, settings — is stored only on your device. None of this data is ever sent to or stored on the developer's servers or any third party's servers. Deleting the App also deletes this data from your device.

3. Location

The App does not access your location.

4. Notifications

All notifications from the App are local notifications generated entirely on your device (reminding you before an estimated reorder date). You can change notification permissions at any time in your device's Settings.

5. Purchases

ReorderIQ is a one-time paid download. Purchase and payment processing is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; the developer never receives your credit card or other payment information. See Apple's own privacy policy for how Apple handles payment data.

6. Third Parties, Analytics & Advertising

The App does not use any third-party analytics tools, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies. Your usage data is never shared with any third party.

7. Children

The App's age rating follows the rating shown on the App Store listing.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be revised as the App's features or applicable law change. If a material change is made, the "Last updated" date on this page will be updated accordingly.
