Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?
Everything is stored only on your device and is never sent anywhere. Deleting the app also deletes your data.

Can I transfer my data to a new phone?
Since data is stored on-device only, it doesn't transfer automatically when you switch phones (a future update may add export/import).

Is ReorderIQ a subscription?
No. ReorderIQ is a one-time purchase — pay once, use it for as long as you like, with no recurring charges.
