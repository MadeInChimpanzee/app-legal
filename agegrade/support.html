Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?
Only on your iPhone. Your personal-best logbook and settings are saved inside the app, never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes them.

Which age-grading tables does the app use?
Road distances (5K, 10K, half marathon, marathon) use the WMA / USATF Masters LDR road age-grading factors. Track events (800 m, 1500 m, mile, 5000 m, 10000 m) use the WMA track & field factors. The exact table version in your build is shown in Settings.

Are the percentages official?
No. They are informational estimates calculated from published tables. They are not official results or rankings, and no race organiser or governing body is involved.

Why is my custom distance marked as an estimate?
Distances that are not in the bundled tables are projected from the nearest bundled distance using the Riegel formula, so they are less authoritative than a direct table lookup. The app labels them clearly for that reason.

Why does the app only cover ages 35 and above?
Age grading is a masters concept, and the published factor tables the app bundles start at 35. Ages outside the supported range are clamped rather than extrapolated.

Why are there only two sex categories?
The published WMA factor tables exist only in male and female forms, so the app can only calculate what those tables define.

Can I move my logbook to a new iPhone?
There is no account or cloud sync, so entries do not transfer automatically. If you are switching phones, note down or share your key results first.

Is this a subscription?
No. Age Grade is a one-time purchase with no in-app purchases and no subscription, so there is nothing to cancel or renew.

I need a refund, or I want to reinstall the app.
Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. Refund requests go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. To reinstall on the same Apple Account, download it again from the App Store or from your purchase history — there is no extra charge.
