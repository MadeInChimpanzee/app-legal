1. What the App Is

The App is an offline calculator and personal-best logbook for masters runners. It converts a race distance and finish time into an age-graded percentage, an age-standard time, and equivalent times at other distances.

2. Results Are Informational Estimates

Age-graded results shown by the App are informational estimates calculated from published WMA / USATF age-grading tables. They are not official results, official rankings, or any form of certification, and they carry no standing with any race organiser or governing body.

  • Road distances use the WMA / USATF Masters LDR road age-grading factors; track events use the WMA track & field factors. Bundled tables reflect a specific published edition and may be superseded when the governing bodies revise them.
  • Equivalent times for distances that are not in the bundled tables are projected with the Riegel formula and are labelled as estimates in the App. Treat them as a rough guide only.
  • Results depend on the age, sex category, distance and time you enter. Course conditions, terrain, elevation, weather and course certification are not taken into account.

The App does not provide medical, coaching or training advice. Consult a qualified professional before starting or changing a training programme.
