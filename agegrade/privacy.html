Privacy Policy

App: Age Grade  /  Last updated: July 31, 2026

Age Grade (the “App”) is designed to work entirely on your device. The App does not collect personal information and does not send your data anywhere. This policy explains how information is handled.

1. Information We Collect

The App collects no personal information — no name, no email address, no phone number, no device identifier. There is no account, no login and no sign-in of any kind.

2. Information You Enter, and Where It Stays

To calculate an age-graded percentage, you enter a race distance, a finish time, an age and a sex category. These values, together with any personal-best entries you save in the logbook and your app settings, are stored only on your device, in the App’s own Application Support folder. They are never uploaded to the developer or to any third party, and they are not linked to your identity. If you delete the App, this data is deleted with it.

3. No Network Connections

The App makes no network requests. The age-grading tables are bundled inside the App, and every calculation runs offline on your device. The App works with no internet connection.

4. No Analytics, Advertising or Tracking

The App contains no third-party analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no tracking technology. No usage data or behavioural data is shared with anyone.

5. Share Cards

The App can render a result as an image. That image is created on your device and passed to the standard iOS share sheet only when you choose to share it. Where it goes from there (Messages, a social app, Files, etc.) is your choice, and is then handled by that app or service under its own terms.

6. Photos, Location and Health Data

The App does not access your photo library, and does not request or use location data, motion data or Health/HealthKit data.

7. Payment

Age Grade is a paid app purchased once from the App Store. There are no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; the developer never receives your credit card number or any other payment details. Apple’s handling of purchase data is described in Apple’s own privacy policy.

8. Children

The App is aimed at adult masters runners and is not directed at children. Its age rating is shown on its App Store listing. Because the App collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be revised as the App changes or as required by law. Any material change will be reflected on this page along with a new “last updated” date.
