Privacy Policy

App: PixelQuilt  /  Last updated: July 31, 2026

PixelQuilt (the “App”) is designed to work entirely on your device. The App does not collect personal information and does not send your data anywhere. This policy explains how information is handled.

1. Information We Collect

The App collects no personal information — no name, no email address, no phone number, no device identifier. There is no account, no login and no sign-in of any kind.

2. Where Your Data Is Stored

Everything you create in the App — saved quilt projects, grid and seam-allowance settings, palette choices and calculated results — is stored only on your device, in the App’s own Application Support folder. It is never uploaded to the developer or to any third party. If you delete the App, this data is deleted with it.

3. Photos

To create a pattern, you choose a photo using the standard iOS photo picker. The selected image is read into the App and processed entirely on your device to build the quilt grid. The App does not browse, scan or index your photo library, and your photos are never transmitted anywhere. You can change photo access at any time in iOS Settings.

4. No Network Connections

The App makes no network requests. All image processing, patch counting and yardage calculation happens offline on your device. The App works with no internet connection.

5. No Analytics, Advertising or Tracking

The App contains no third-party analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no tracking technology. No usage data or behavioural data is shared with anyone.

6. Exporting and Sharing

When you export a cutting-guide PDF, the file is generated on your device and handed to the standard iOS share sheet only when you choose to share it. Where the file goes from there (Mail, Files, AirDrop, a printer, etc.) is your choice, and is then handled by that app or service under its own terms.

7. Location

The App does not request or use location data.

8. Payment

PixelQuilt is a paid app purchased once from the App Store. There are no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; the developer never receives your credit card number or any other payment details. Apple’s handling of purchase data is described in Apple’s own privacy policy.

9. Children

The App is a general-purpose hobby tool and is not directed at children. Its age rating is shown on its App Store listing. Because the App collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be revised as the App changes or as required by law. Any material change will be reflected on this page along with a new “last updated” date.
