Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?
Only on your iPhone. Projects and settings are saved inside the app, never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes them.

Do my photos leave my device?
No. The photo you pick is processed on your device to build the grid, and is never sent anywhere.

Can I move my projects to a new iPhone?
There is no account or cloud sync, so projects do not transfer automatically. Exporting your cutting-guide PDFs and keeping them in Files or iCloud Drive is the reliable way to preserve a project.

Are the yardage numbers exact?
They are estimates, deliberately on the safe side. The App assumes 42 inches of usable fabric width, lays patches out in simple rows rather than an optimised layout, and rounds up to the nearest 1/8 yard. Fat-quarter numbers are an area-based approximation. Always check against your actual fabric before buying.

Why does my pattern look blocky or lose detail?
Every patch is a single solid colour, so fine detail disappears at small grid sizes. Try increasing the grid size, or cropping tighter on the subject before generating the pattern.

How do I get the cutting guide?
Open a finished pattern and use the export option to generate a PDF containing the grid chart and the per-colour cutting list, then share or save it via the iOS share sheet.

Is this a subscription?
No. PixelQuilt is a one-time purchase with no in-app purchases and no subscription, so there is nothing to cancel or renew.

I need a refund, or I want to reinstall the app.
Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. Refund requests go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. To reinstall on the same Apple Account, download it again from the App Store or from your purchase history — there is no extra charge.
