1. What the App Is

The App is an offline planning tool for quilters. It converts a photo you choose into a pieced quilt grid and estimates patch counts, fabric requirements and cutting information. It is a planning aid, not a guarantee of any particular result.

2. Estimates — Verify Before Buying Fabric

Yardage and material figures produced by the App are estimates. Please check them against your own project before purchasing fabric. In particular:

  • Fabric is assumed to have a usable width of 42 inches (a common allowance for quilting cotton after trimming selvages). Your actual fabric may be narrower or wider.
  • Patches are laid out in simple rows across that width, not with optimal 2D nesting, so the result is intentionally conservative rather than exact.
  • Yardage is rounded up to the nearest 1/8 yard, the usual quilt-shop cutting unit.
  • Fat-quarter figures are an area-based approximation, not a cutting layout.

Cutting errors, shrinkage, directional prints, fussy cutting and fabric defects are not accounted for. You remain responsible for your own cutting and purchasing decisions.
