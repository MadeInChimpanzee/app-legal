App: EarType　/　Last updated: July 14, 2026

EarType (the "App") is designed with respect for your privacy. As a matter of principle, the App does not collect any personal information and does not send any data externally. This policy explains how the App handles information.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect any personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number. No account or sign-in is required.

2. Where Data Is Stored

Any data you enter or that the App records, along with your settings, is stored only on your device. This data is never transmitted to or stored on the developer's or any third party's servers. Deleting the App removes this data from your device.

3. Location

The App does not access your location.

4. In-App Purchases

Purchases of paid features are processed through Apple's In-App Purchase (StoreKit). Payment is handled by Apple, and the developer never receives your payment information such as credit card numbers. Please refer to Apple's privacy policy regarding purchases.

5. Third Parties, Analytics, and Ads

The App uses no third-party analytics tools, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies. Your usage data is never shared with third parties.

6. Children

The App's age rating follows the rating shown on the App Store.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as features change or as required by law. When there are significant changes, the "Last updated" date on this page will be revised.
