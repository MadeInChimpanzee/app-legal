Privacy Policy

App: MisoCalm  /  Last updated: October 23, 2026

MisoCalm (the “App”) is designed to work entirely on your device. The App does not collect personal information and does not send your data anywhere. This policy explains how information is handled.

1. Information We Collect

The App collects no personal information — no name, no email address, no phone number, no device identifier. There is no account, no login and no sign-in of any kind.

2. Where Your Data Is Stored

Everything you create in the App — your trigger sounds, training sessions, discomfort ratings, imported music and settings — is stored only on your device, in the App’s own Application Support folder. It is never uploaded to the developer or to any third party. If you delete the App, this data is deleted with it.

3. Network Connections

The App connects to Apple (the App Store) only to process and restore the in-app purchase. Masking, training, recording and progress all work offline on your device, and your sounds and records are never transmitted.

4. No Analytics, Advertising or Tracking

The App contains no third-party analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no tracking technology. No usage data or behavioural data is shared with anyone.

Microphone

The App uses the microphone only when you tap Record to capture a trigger sound for training. The recording is saved only on your device and is never uploaded. You can change microphone access at any time in iOS Settings, or import a sound file instead.

Exporting and Sharing

When you export a file, it is generated on your device and handed to the standard iOS share sheet only when you choose to share it. Where the file goes from there (Mail, Files, AirDrop, a printer, etc.) is your choice, and is then handled by that app or service under its own terms.

5. Photos, Location and Other Sensitive Data

The App does not access your photo library, contacts or camera, and does not request or use location data or Health data. Audio files you import are copied into the App’s own folder on your device.

6. Payment

MisoCalm is free to download. The optional one-time purchase (MisoCalm Pro) is processed by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). There is no subscription. Payment is handled entirely by Apple, and the developer never receives your credit card number or any other payment details. Apple’s handling of purchase data is described in Apple’s own privacy policy.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children. Its age rating is shown on its App Store listing. Because the App collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be revised as the App changes or as required by law. Any material change will be reflected on this page along with a new “last updated” date.
