MisoCalm is a self-help tool for people who find certain everyday sounds distressing (often called misophonia). It plays masking noise and lets you listen to your own trigger sounds at a low volume that you choose. It is provided for general wellness and reference only.

Protect your hearing. Use the lowest volume that works, take breaks, and avoid long listening at high volume. You can set a maximum volume for the mask in Settings.
